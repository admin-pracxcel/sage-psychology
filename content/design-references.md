# Design references

The client is expecting a website at the level of craft these sites show. Fetch each one before designing anything.

- https://sereenity.framer.website/
- https://holistic.framer.media/
- https://mindoor.framer.website/
- https://claritycare.framer.website/
- https://sunmoon.framer.ai/
- https://kosmetisk.framer.website/
- https://healyra.framer.website/

## What these share

Modern wellness-editorial aesthetic. Confident hero moments with real typographic personality. Warm, considered palettes with rich colour work inside a restrained range. Generous whitespace paired with imagery-forward layouts. Ambient scroll-driven motion, hover micro-interactions, thoughtful section transitions. Soft rounded geometry where it appears. Type as personality: display faces with real character paired deliberately with legible body faces.

## What to take from them

Real typography direction, not defaults. A hero that establishes tone in the first paint. Ambient motion that supports the content (slow reveals, parallax with restraint, image blur-in, gentle scale) rather than performs. Sectional rhythm with variety, no single repeated block pattern. Confident use of imagery. Layouts that feel authored rather than assembled.

## What to avoid

Stock therapy imagery, hands on shoulders, silhouettes at sunset, monochrome sad faces, therapy-couch clichés. Sales-page energy, giant urgent CTAs, countdown timers, big red buttons. Anything that reads as a templated agency site or a generic wellness Squarespace. The two colour signals from the client are green and neutral tones, so pick a real position inside that direction rather than a third accent.

## Photography

Real photography of Jacob and the East Fremantle practice will be supplied later. Build in ways that give real imagery room to breathe when it arrives. Until then, use restrained abstract, botanical, or architectural imagery. Never use an image implying a clinical outcome, which is its own compliance risk.

## The bar

The client will approve on sight if the site feels crafted, modern, and calm at once. The reference sites hit that combination. Match it or exceed it.
