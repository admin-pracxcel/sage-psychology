**SECTION 1, Empathy + understanding anxiety (H2)**

**Understanding Anxiety**

Anxiety is one of the most common reasons people seek psychological support, and one of the most treatable. In its everyday form, anxiety is a normal and even useful response that keeps us alert to genuine threats. It becomes a problem when it lingers, intensifies, and begins to intrude on daily life, when worry becomes constant, when the body stays braced for danger that never comes, or when panic strikes seemingly out of nowhere.

If any of that sounds familiar, you are not alone, and you are not overreacting. Anxiety can be understood, and it can be managed. At Sage Psychological Services, registered psychologist <u>Jacob Jones</u> works with adults to make sense of anxiety and to build practical, lasting ways of managing it.

**SECTION 2, Signs & forms of anxiety (H2, entity + long-tail)**

**How Anxiety Can Show Up**

Anxiety looks different from person to person. You might recognise some of the following:

-   Persistent, hard-to-control worry about everyday things

-   A racing heart, tight chest, shortness of breath, or restlessness

-   Panic attacks that arrive suddenly and feel overwhelming

-   Trouble sleeping because your mind will not settle

-   Avoiding situations, places, or people that trigger unease

-   A constant sense of dread or feeling permanently on edge

-   Difficulty concentrating or a mind that feels perpetually busy

Anxiety also takes more specific forms, including generalised anxiety, social anxiety, panic disorder, and health anxiety. Whatever shape yours takes, therapy can help you understand what is driving it and what will help.

**SECTION 3, How therapy helps (H2, links to CBT treatment)**

**How Therapy Helps with Anxiety**

The most effective anxiety treatment is evidence-based and tailored to the individual. Jacob most often draws on <u>Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT)</u>, which is considered a first-line treatment for anxiety. CBT helps you identify the thoughts and behaviours that fuel anxiety, gently test the fears driving it, and build practical strategies for calming both mind and body.

Where anxiety is rooted in past experiences or trauma, Jacob may also incorporate <u>EMDR</u> or psychodynamic approaches to address the underlying causes rather than the symptoms alone. As always, the approach is shaped around you, and Jacob will explain what is likely to help during your first session.

**SECTION 4, What you can expect (H2, hope without guarantees, AHPRA-safe)**

**A Calmer Relationship with Your Thoughts**

Therapy for anxiety is not about eliminating every worry, which no one can promise. It is about changing your relationship with anxiety so that it no longer runs the show. Many clients find that, with the right support, they can quieten the noise, face the situations they had been avoiding, and move through daily life with far more ease and confidence. The work is collaborative, paced to suit you, and grounded in approaches with strong evidence behind them.
