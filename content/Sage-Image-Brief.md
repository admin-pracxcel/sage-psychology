# Sage Psychological Services — Image Brief

**Revised edition.** Warm and forward-looking tone throughout, with every AI-generated image structured so no identifiable human face is produced.

8 pages · 75 image slots · 65 AI-generated · 10 to be photographed for real · Prepared 24 July 2026

---

## How to use this document

**Every prompt is complete.** Realism, face-safety, skin-texture and negative instructions are already merged into each one. Copy the block and paste it. Nothing needs appending or assembling.

**Do not add anything to the prompts.** No camera models, no "hyper-realistic", "cinematic", "8K" or "masterpiece". Those words pull the output toward the plastic AI look. The length and the mundane detail are what keep it grounded.

**Generate several and keep the worst-composed one.** Run each prompt four or five times. The usable frame is usually the one that looks slightly badly taken — off-centre, something blocking a corner, focus marginally missed. The tidy, well-lit result is the one that reads as AI.

**Degrade before publishing.** Even a good generation is too clean. In any editor: add 2–4% monochrome noise, drop global saturation 8–12%, lift the blacks a few points so shadows aren't pure, apply a very slight unsharp mask rather than heavy sharpening, then export as JPEG at quality 82–88 rather than maximum. Real web photos carry compression artefacts; AI output doesn't unless you add them.

**If something still looks synthetic, don't describe the subject more.** The fix is environmental: push the subject further from the camera, put something in front of them, remove the flattering light, add more ordinary clutter.

### Why no faces

Every AI prompt in this document forces the person to be seen from behind, in profile turned away, cropped, obscured, or small in the frame. This is deliberate on two counts. It removes any AHPRA exposure around images that could be read as depicting real clients or implying testimonials, since a synthetic face can't be disclosed as a model. And it happens to be the single most reliable way to avoid the uncanny-valley tell, because faces are where generated images fail most visibly. The images stay unmistakably human — hands, backs, postures, people mid-movement — without ever producing a recognisable person.

### What to photograph for real

10 slots are marked **SHOOT FOR REAL** rather than prompted. These are Jacob's portrait and the practice interiors. The consulting room does more persuasive work than anything else on the site — it answers "what am I walking into?" — and a generated room can't do that job honestly. All of it is one half-day with a photographer at 7B Silas Street, and the same visit covers every interior slot across all eight pages.
